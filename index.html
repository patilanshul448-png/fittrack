Warm-up & cooldown

Warm-up · 5–7 min

March in place, arm circles, hip hinges, gentle squats and shoulder mobility. Add 10 warm-up push-ups Tue–Fri only if comfortable; optional on tired/sore days. Monday is the dedicated push day.

Cooldown · ~5 min

Chest stretch, shoulder stretch, quad stretch, hamstring stretch, child’s pose. Hold each comfortably for 20–30 seconds.

Stop for sharp pain, dizziness or joint pain. Keep most sets 1–2 reps short of failure. Never train through pain.

Safe progression

Only add 1–2 reps per set when form stays good, or slow reps down. Skip or shorten workouts rather than sacrificing sleep.

Face, eyes & ears

Simple daily care for skin, posture, vision comfort and hearing protection.

~10–15 min + screen breaks

Face & skin care

Keep the routine gentle. These habits support skin health; they do not reshape facial bones or guarantee a sharper jawline.

Morning: gentle cleanser, non-comedogenic moisturiser and broad-spectrum SPF 30–50. Evening: gentle cleanse and moisturise. Use lip balm; SPF lip balm outdoors. Don't pick pimples or use harsh scrubs.

Neck & jaw comfort

Posture and relaxation only — no forceful jaw or neck training.

Keep your head comfortable and neutral, relax your jaw with teeth slightly apart at rest, and avoid jaw trainers or excessive gum chewing. Stop neck movements if painful or dizzy.

Eye protection

For spectacle wearers and screen-related eye tiredness.

Follow the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look about 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds. Use your prescribed spectacles and comfortable lighting. Eye exercises do not reduce spectacle power.

Ear & hearing protection

Protect hearing and avoid irritating the ear canal.

Clean only the outer ear with a soft cloth. Never insert cotton buds or tools into the canal; avoid ear candles and unadvised drops. Keep headphone volume moderate and take breaks.

Tinnitus check-in

You previously reported ringing in your ears. Record what you notice; this tracker cannot diagnose the cause.

Please tell a parent or trusted adult and arrange a doctor/ENT assessment for the ringing, including an ear examination and hearing test if advised. Sudden hearing loss with ringing needs urgent medical care. Also seek prompt care for heartbeat-synchronous ringing, severe dizziness or sudden neurological symptoms. Don't try to clear deep wax yourself.
